Travel Ajo, one step at a time.

The supplied brief describes Travel Ajo as a flexible travel-savings programme that lets participants build a balance over time and later apply it to eligible travel purchases.

  • Minimum contribution stated as ₦100,000 per payment.
  • Contribute at a frequency that suits you, subject to programme terms.
  • The brief states 0% interest and no administrative financing charges.
  • A ₦10,000 discount is stated for eligible standard packages cleared using an active Travel Ajo balance.
  • The brief says the balance can be used across eligible packages and travel products.
